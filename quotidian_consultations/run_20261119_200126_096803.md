# Estimated monthly cost of JEV for article screening

I couldn't open the linked page, so this uses the pricing you gave: **$0.042 per million input tokens, output free**. The token counts are my assumptions, and the estimate is only as good as they are.

## 1. Pricing

| Item | Rate |
|---|---|
| Input | $0.042 / 1M tokens, or **$0.000000042 per token** |
| Output | $0 |

## 2. Assumptions

| Parameter | Value | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Articles per week | 5,000 | Given |
| Characters per article | 6,000 | Given (about 900–1,000 words) |
| Characters per token | about 4 | Typical for English prose |
| **Article tokens** | **about 1,500** | 6,000 ÷ 4 |
| Prompt overhead per call | about 300 tokens | Dataset definition, label options, instructions (my guess) |
| **Input tokens per article** | **about 1,800** | |
| Output tokens | Negligible | Billed at $0 |
| Weeks per month | 4.33 | 52 ÷ 12 |

## 3. Volume

| Period | Articles | Input tokens (1,800 each) |
|---|---|---|
| Per article | 1 | 1,800 |

## 4. Cost (baseline)

| Metric | Cost |
|---|---|
| Per token | $0.000000042 |
| Per article | 1,800 × $0.000000042 = **$0.0000756** (about 0.0076¢) |
| Per 1,000 articles | **$0.076** |
| Per week | 9.0 M × $0.042/M = **$0.378** |
| **Per month** | 39.0 M × $0.042/M = **about $1.64** |
| Per year | about $19.66 |

## 5. Sensitivity

| Scenario | Tokens per article | Monthly tokens | Monthly cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low (short text, minimal prompt) | 1,300 | 28.2 M | **$1.18** |
| **Baseline** | 1,800 | 39.0 M | **$1.64** |
| High (dense text or non-English, long prompt) | 2,500 | 54.2 M | **$2.28** |
| Baseline, 5 datasets checked per article | 9,000 | 195 M | **$8.19** |
| Baseline, 10 datasets checked per article | 18,000 | 390 M | **$16.38** |

Even at 10 datasets per article, the cost is under $20 a month.

## 6. Other relevant metrics

- **Cost per decision:** about $0.00008 per article per dataset, or roughly 13,000 article classifications per $1.
- **Output cost:** zero, so you can request richer outputs (labels, confidence scores, evidence tags) without paying for them. This matters because LLM pricing usually penalizes output tokens.
- **Scaling:** cost is linear. Doubling to 10,000 articles per week gives about $3.28 a month at baseline.
- **Comparison with an LLM (illustrative):** a small LLM at about $0.15/M input and $0.60/M output, with about 50 output tokens per decision, would cost about $0.0003 per article, or about $6.50 a month. That is roughly 4× JEV for the same volume. Frontier LLMs would be 50–100× more.
- **Likely larger costs:** fetching and cleaning articles, storage, and human review of borderline cases will probably exceed the JEV cost by a wide margin.

## 7. Things to verify

1. **Tokenizer:** JEV may count tokens differently than the 4-characters-per-token rule. Run a sample of 100 articles and check the billed tokens.
2. **Prompt overhead:** if JEV takes a long dataset description or many labels per call, overhead could exceed 300 tokens. Even 2,000 tokens of overhead would only raise the monthly cost to about $3.50.
3. **Truncation limits:** check JEV's maximum context length. If articles are truncated or chunked, token counts change.
4. **Minimums and rate limits:** the page may list minimum spend or throughput limits that affect actual billing.

**Bottom line:** at 5,000 articles per week, expect about **$1.60 a month** (a realistic range of $1.20–$2.30), or about $0.38 a week.
