## 6. Cost multipliers

- **Several datasets per article:** If each article is scored against N datasets, cost scales roughly linearly with N, unless the model lets you reuse the article encoding. Base case × 10 datasets ≈ **$16.40/month**, and × 50 datasets ≈ $82/month.
- **Re-processing:** Re-running articles after changing a dataset definition adds a full pass each time, about $1.64 per pass at this volume.
- **Raw HTML:** Sending HTML instead of visible text can multiply tokens by 3–10×. Strip it first.
- **Volume growth:** Cost scales linearly. 100,000 articles/week would be about $33/month in the base case.

## 7. Other relevant metrics

- **Break-even against an LLM:** An LLM costing, say, $0.50 per MTok input and $2 per MTok output would cost roughly 12× more on input alone, plus output charges. The exact gap depends on the model you compare against.
- **Cost per positive:** If ~5% of articles qualify as evidence (about 1,083 per month), the classification cost is **~$0.0015 per confirmed evidence article**.
- **Non-JEV costs** will likely dominate: crawling and monitoring, storage, deduplication, human review of borderline or positive items, and engineering time.

## Bottom line

At 5,000 articles/week, expect roughly **$1.40–$2.30 per month** (base case about **$1.64**) for one dataset. Even with dozens of datasets or a worst-case token count, it stays in the tens of dollars per month. To tighten the estimate, run a sample of 100 real articles through the tokenizer, record the average input tokens including your prompt, and plug it into:

**Monthly cost = articles/month × tokens per article × $0.000000042**
